# Ties: Countback on Grand Prix races, then Latest-best

Riders level on Championship points are separated by Countback on Grand Prix race finishes only (most wins, then most second places, …), ignoring Sprints, then by the Latest-best tie-break (the Rider whose highest place came later ranks ahead), as in FIM Grand Prix Sporting Regulations art. 1.28.7. Final Standings therefore have no shared ranks, and a rival who can at best tie on points but would lose the tie-break can no longer catch the leader when deciding the World Champion.

We apply this rule to every era as an assumption: we have not verified the tie-break wording of older regulations. If a historical Season turns out to have broken ties differently, the tie-break becomes part of the Scoring rule.
