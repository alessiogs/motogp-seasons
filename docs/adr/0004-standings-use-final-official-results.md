# Standings use final official Results; Dropped scores only look backwards

The Standings at any Step are built from the final official Classification of every Session up to that Step: a later disqualification or penalty (e.g. Ogura, Argentina 2025) is applied to the Session it belongs to, not on the date it was decided. We chose this over reconstructing what was known on each date, which would need a history of every correction and an effective date for each.

Dropped scores, by contrast, are applied progressively: after Round k, each Counting window considers only Rounds 1..k, so mid-season Standings match the championship as it actually stood rather than with end-of-season drops applied early. When two Round scores tie for being dropped, the earlier Round is the one marked as dropped.
